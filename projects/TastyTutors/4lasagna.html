Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish of pasta sheets alternating with fillings, such as cheese, ragù, and besciamella, and baked. The word "lasagna" can also refer to the type of wide, flat pasta sheet used to make the dish. Lasagna recipes have been around since the 13th century, and today there are many varieties, including cream sauces, veggies, and traditional recipes.

Ingredients

For the Meat Sauce:

  • 1 pound (450g) lean ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (6 ounces) tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

For the Cheese Mixture:

  • 15 ounces (425g) ricotta cheese
  • 1 cup (100g) grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Additional Ingredients:

  • 12 lasagna noodles, cooked according to package instructions
  • 2 cups (200g) shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add chopped onion and garlic, and sauté until softened, about 3-4 minutes.
  2. Add ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up with a spoon as it cooks.
  3. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, dried oregano, dried basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens. Remove from heat and set aside.
  4. In a mixing bowl, combine ricotta cheese, grated Parmesan cheese, egg, chopped parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix until well combined. Set aside.
  5. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  7. Place a layer of cooked lasagna noodles over the sauce, overlapping slightly.
  8. Spread half of the cheese mixture evenly over the noodles.
  9. Spoon half of the remaining meat sauce over the cheese mixture.
  10. Sprinkle half of the shredded mozzarella cheese over the meat sauce.
  11. Repeat the layers: noodles, cheese mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese.
  12. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and bake in the preheated oven for 30 minutes.
  13. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 15-20 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  14. Remove the lasagna from the oven and let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
  15. Serve hot, garnished with additional chopped parsley if desired.
